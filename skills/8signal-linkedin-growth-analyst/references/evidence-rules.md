# Evidence rules

Use these labels in conclusions:

- **Observed:** a visible profile field, full post, date, or count with a direct URL or identified export row.
- **Inference:** a plausible interpretation of observed patterns, naming alternatives and sample limitations.
- **Experiment:** a proposed change whose effect remains unmeasured.

Visible reactions + comments + reposts are an interaction count, not a reach-normalized engagement rate. Missing metrics are not zero. A post's public total also depends on its age, audience size, and collection timing. A cross-format comparison with few posts, incomplete counts, or different periods is exploratory.

Never state private metrics or feed-ranking mechanics as if measured. Avoid fixed posting-time prescriptions, guaranteed gains, fabricated post quotations, and claims that a pattern caused a result. Include the number of posts behind every comparative claim and link representative examples. Note when the collection method may exclude posts.
